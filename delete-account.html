Delete My Account & Data

Effective: September 5, 2026

You can permanently delete your DueBack account and all data associated with it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open DueBack and go to Settings.
  2. Under Cloud Account & Sync, tap Delete My Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your synced receipts (including images), and your purchase records from our systems.

Option 2 — Without the app

If you no longer have DueBack installed, email privacy@bobbycodes.dev from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my DueBack account." We will process the request and confirm by email within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • All synced receipts, including receipt images and extracted text
  • Your purchase/Lifetime Pro record on our systems

What isn't deleted by us

  • If you used local-only mode (no account), your data was never sent to us — deleting the app from your device removes it completely, and there's nothing further to request here.
  • Google Play or Apple may separately retain their own transaction records of any purchase you made, per their own policies — we don't control or have access to delete those.
