Privacy Policy

Effective: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DueBack ("the App," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. DueBack is developed by BobbyCodes.

1. Data DueBack collects

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account email. If you create an account (optional — see below), we collect the email address you sign up with, via our backend provider Supabase Auth.
  • Receipt data. When you scan or add a receipt, DueBack captures a photo of the receipt and extracts text from it (merchant name, price, purchase date, and any return/warranty/trial deadlines) using on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). This processing happens locally on your device — the receipt image is not sent anywhere for text extraction.

1.2 Local-only mode vs. cloud sync

DueBack works fully offline without an account. In that mode, all receipt data — including photos and extracted text — is stored only in a local database on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

If you choose to sign in, your receipts (including images, merchant names, prices, dates, and deadlines) are additionally synced to our backend (Supabase) so you can back them up and access them if you reinstall the app. This sync is optional and off by default.

1.3 Purchase information

DueBack offers a one-time "Lifetime Pro" unlock, processed by Google Play Billing (or Apple's In-App Purchase on iOS). We store a record of the purchase (product ID, an order/transaction identifier, and status) linked to your account so your Pro status can be restored across devices. We do not receive or store your payment card details — that is handled entirely by Google/Apple.

1.4 Automatically collected information

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and DueBack does not collect device identifiers, location, or browsing/usage analytics.

2. How we use data

  • To provide the app's core function: extracting and tracking return/warranty/trial deadlines from your receipts.
  • To sync and back up your data across devices, if you opt in by signing in.
  • To validate and restore your Lifetime Pro purchase.
  • To send local reminder notifications about upcoming deadlines — these are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and do not require any data to leave your phone.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-party service providers

We use the following service providers to operate the app. Each processes data only as necessary to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and file storage for account data, synced receipts, and purchase records, when you use cloud sync.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — processes the Lifetime Pro purchase.
  • Google ML Kit (on-device) — performs text recognition locally on your device; images are not uploaded to Google for this purpose.

4. Data security

Synced data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Our database enforces row-level security so that each account can only read or modify its own data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data retention

We retain synced account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete a receipt, it is removed from sync on your next successful sync. If you delete your account (see Delete My Account & Data), your synced data is permanently deleted.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & export. You can export all your receipt data to a CSV file at any time from within the app.
  • Deletion. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time — see Delete My Account & Data.
  • Local-only use. You can use DueBack without ever creating an account, in which case no data is sent to us at all.

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or a U.S. state with its own privacy law (such as California), you may have additional rights (e.g., to access, correct, or object to processing of your data). Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

7. Children's privacy

DueBack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than your own. By using DueBack's cloud sync features, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective" date above when we do. Material changes will be noted within the app.
